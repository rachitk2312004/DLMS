Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data when using our **Event & Duty Leave Management System**.

Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Name, university ID (UID), email, and role (e.g., student, coordinator) when registering.
  • Account Security: Encrypted passwords and OTP verification for authentication.
  • Leave & Event Data: Leave requests, approval status, event participation, time slots, and remarks.
  • Usage & Logs: Activity timestamps, leave request history, and attendance updates.

How We Use Your Information

  • To facilitate duty leave requests, approvals, and tracking.
  • To manage event registrations and attendance efficiently.
  • To ensure security, prevent misuse, and enforce authentication.
  • To provide real-time updates and notifications regarding application status.

Data Security & Retention

We implement strict security measures to protect your data. Personal information and activity logs are stored securely and are only retained as long as necessary for academic and administrative purposes.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or request the deletion of your information. For any concerns, contact us at eventanddutyleave.ms@gmail.com.
